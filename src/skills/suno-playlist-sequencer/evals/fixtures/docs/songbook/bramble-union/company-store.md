# Company Store

## Theme

Debt to the company store as a cage: anger at a wage that comes back to the people who paid it. Defiant, working-class rage.

## Lyrics (excerpt)

Paid in scrip and paid in sweat / Paid till there's nothing left / But the ledger says I owe them yet

## Listening Notes

Driving electric folk-rock, 112 BPM, A minor.
