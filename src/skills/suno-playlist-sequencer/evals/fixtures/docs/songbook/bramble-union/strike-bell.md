# Strike Bell

## Theme

The union rings the bell and the mill goes silent. An anthem of collective nerve, the album's peak of defiance.

## Lyrics (excerpt)

Ring it once for the ones who fell / Ring it twice for the company's hell / Ring it till they hear the strike bell

## Listening Notes

Full band, pounding floor toms, 132 BPM, C minor.
