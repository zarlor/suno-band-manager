The Weir Keeper

Every morning at the weir
    I lift the boards
        and let the river through
my father did it   and his father   too


Hold   the   gate
    hold   the   gate
the water does not care whose hands are on the wheel

In the flood year the boards were swollen shut
    I broke my knuckles on the iron
        and the town slept on
not one of them   knew   my name


Hold   the   gate
    hold   the   gate
the water does not care whose hands are on the wheel

When I am gone   somebody else will learn
    which board sticks
        which one sings
and the river will go on   standing tall   without me
