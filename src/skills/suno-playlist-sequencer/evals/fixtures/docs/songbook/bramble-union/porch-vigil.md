# Porch Vigil

## Theme

A mother on the porch every evening, waiting for a letter from her son overseas. The letters stop. The song ends before any news comes.

## Lyrics (excerpt)

Lamp lit, screen door latched / Mailman's late again / I keep his supper warm / Like I can keep him then

## Listening Notes

Sparse piano and brushed snare, 72 BPM, E minor drifting to B minor at the end.
