# Saturday Payday

## Theme

A straight-up party song: the mill whistle blows on payday and the whole town spills into the tavern. No subtext; it is exactly the good time it sounds like.

## Lyrics (excerpt)

Whistle's blowin', pockets full / Fiddle's tuned and the floor is cool / Spend it all 'fore Sunday school

## Listening Notes

Fiddle and stomp-box hoedown, 98 BPM, D major, gang vocals on the chorus.
