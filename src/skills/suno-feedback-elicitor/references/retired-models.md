# Retired Models (retired 2026-09-09)

Read only when the take under discussion was made on a pre-v6 model. Retired models can't generate any more, so recommendations still target a current model; these notes explain why an older take behaved as it did. Treat each claim as history until re-confirmed on v6.

- **v4 Pro:** a hard 200-character style-prompt limit, silently truncated, so anything past it never reached the model; broad genre and mood words worked better than nuance; no sliders, no Persona.
- **v4.5-all (Free):** limited vocal control without a Persona; conversational, descriptive prompts worked; no sliders, so everything went through the prompt and exclusions; regenerating ("make again") was the first move before a prompt change.
- **v4.5 Pro / v4.5+ Pro:** v4.5-all's prompting with sliders and Personas; sliders came before a longer prompt; v4.5+ improved section-level control; a Persona locked vocal direction more reliably than the prompt alone.
- **v5 Pro:** better vocal nuance; crisp, concise descriptors; section-level editing; responded well to production-quality descriptors ("punchy drums, wide stereo field, crisp high-end, warm bass"); BPM and key in the style prompt (not the lyrics) were worth a try ("deep house, 122 BPM, A minor, hypnotic groove"), unconfirmed. The Weirdness observations (50 too steady for multi-tempo songs, 60 better, 65 best) were made on v5 Pro at Style Influence 70.
- **v5.5:** brought the generate → inspect → section-replace → refine workflow and the web Duration slider. Late in a song, vocals were reported losing timbre in the 2–4 minute range (2026-08, mild); the advice was sub-2:00 segments stitched together, or Replace Section on the late material.
- **The "live" crowd-noise trigger** was confirmed on v5.5 and still applies.
