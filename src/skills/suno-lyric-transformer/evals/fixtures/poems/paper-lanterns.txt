Paper Lanterns

We walked down to the river after dark
the whole town carrying its small lights
灯籠を水に浮かべて
my grandmother's name in brushstrokes on the side
she said the river knows the way back home
川は覚えている

The current took them slow at first
then all at once, a hundred tiny suns
ゆらゆら揺れて
Obon night, お盆の夜, the taiko still going
I tried to keep my eyes on hers
さようなら、おばあちゃん

Next summer I will write my own name small
underneath hers, in the corner, the way she taught me
灯りは消えても
the river keeps on carrying
what we were too afraid to hold
川は覚えている
