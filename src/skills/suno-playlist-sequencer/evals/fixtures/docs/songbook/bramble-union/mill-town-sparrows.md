# Mill Town Sparrows

## Theme

Kids growing up in the shadow of the mill, already knowing which of them will leave (the army, the city) and which will stay. Wistful, affectionate; it plants the boy who enlists.

## Lyrics (excerpt)

Tommy said he'd see the ocean / Uncle Sam would pay the fare / We were sparrows on the wire / Never thought the wire'd be bare

## Listening Notes

Acoustic guitar and harmonica, 104 BPM, B minor.
