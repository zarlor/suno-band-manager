# Black Lung Lullaby

## Theme

A daughter singing her dying father to sleep; the cough in the next room is the mine's last word. Grief before the loss.

## Lyrics (excerpt)

Hush now, the shift is done / Hush now, you're the only one / Coughing coal into the sheets

## Listening Notes

Solo voice and fingerpicked guitar, 66 BPM, D minor.
