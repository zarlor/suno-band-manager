# Sweetwater Dance

## Theme

A courtship at the county dance; two shy kids finally dance. Genuinely happy, the album's lightest moment besides the party.

## Lyrics (excerpt)

You stepped on my boots and I didn't mind / Sweetwater lights and the summertime

## Listening Notes

Swing two-step, 120 BPM, G major, accordion lead.
