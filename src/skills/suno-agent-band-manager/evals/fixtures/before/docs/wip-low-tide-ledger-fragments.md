# Low Tide Ledger — workshop fragments

> **Status:** workshopping the second verse. Band: Tidewater Rail. Chorus is locked; verse 2 still searching for its turn.

## Current draft

```
[Verse 1]
Mama kept a ledger by the kitchen door
Every flood we lived through, every debt we swore
Tide came in and took the porch, the tide went out again
She wrote it down in pencil so she'd know where we had been

[Chorus]
Low tide, low tide
Show me what the water hides
Every line I never paid
Waiting on the riverside
```
