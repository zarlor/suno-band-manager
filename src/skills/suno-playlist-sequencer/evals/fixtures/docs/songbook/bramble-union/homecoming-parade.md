# Homecoming Parade

## Theme

Despite the title, this is not a celebration. It is a funeral: the boy from "Mill Town Sparrows" comes home from the war in a flag-draped coffin, and the town lines Mercer Street to watch the hearse pass. The brass and the march snare are a funeral band, not a victory parade. The bright major key is the irony the writer wanted: the town plays the only tune it knows for a homecoming because there is no tune for this one.

Writer's note: "Everyone hears the horns and thinks it's the Fourth of July. Then they hear the second verse. That's the point."

## Lyrics (excerpt)

Verse 1:
The whole town turned out to bring him home
Hats in their hands on Mercer Street
The band struck up the only song they know
For a boy who left on his own two feet

Verse 2:
Six men walking where he used to run
Stars and stripes where his shoulders were
His mother on the porch she never left
Waiting for the letter that came with him

Chorus:
Homecoming, homecoming
Play it loud so she won't hear
Homecoming, homecoming
The parade is for the ones still here

## Listening Notes

Brass band and march snare over acoustic guitar, 96 BPM, D major. Bright and stately on the surface; the vocal is restrained, almost spoken in verse 2.
